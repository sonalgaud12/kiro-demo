# Product: LoomTrack

LoomTrack is an order tracker for a small powerloom unit near Coimbatore (about 40 looms).

## Users
- The mill owner and his floor supervisor. Neither is technical.
- Buyers are garment exporters in Tiruppur. They place orders by phone or WhatsApp. They do not log in to LoomTrack.

## Problems it solves
- "Where is my order?" calls: the owner should see each order's progress at a glance.
- Orders that silently fall behind: the app must warn before a delivery date is missed.
- Disputes about how many metres were sent: every dispatch is recorded and can never exceed what was woven.

## Product rules
- Quantities are in metres and may have decimals (for example 120.5 m).
- Screens must be simple. Every label is shown in both Tamil and English, for example "ஆர்டர்கள் / Orders".
- Keep the first version small: orders, daily production, dispatches and delay warnings only.
